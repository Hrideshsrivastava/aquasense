About AquaSense

From purifiers to intelligent water ecosystems.

AquaSense adds a component-level digital twin to connected purifiers. It scores each part, forecasts remaining useful life with a confidence range, and cross-checks signals before naming a cause, so sensor faults are not mistaken for wear.

Prototype notes: this demo runs rule-based scoring in your browser on synthetic data. The full design uses XGBoost, Isolation Forest and SHAP behind a FastAPI service. RUL is an estimate, not a water-safety certification.
